geNomad: NN classification

Neural-network class scores for each sequence in the human gut assembly.

Source: human_gut_assembly_nn_classification.tsv — 27,617 rows

The neural-network classifier assigns each assembly sequence a probability for each of the three geNomad classes. The three scores sum to ~1, so the highest score is the predicted class — a quick ranking, not a hard call. In this assembly the argmax is chromosome for 13,159 sequences, plasmid for 8,649 and virus for 5,809. Click any score header to sort and find the most confident calls.
