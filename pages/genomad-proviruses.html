geNomad: proviruses

Integrated (proviral) viral sequences in the human gut assembly.

Source: human_gut_assembly_provirus.tsv — 30 rows

What this shows. geNomad's find-proviruses module scans every assembly contig for integrated (proviral) viral sequences. Each row is one provirus. This run of the human-gut assembly found 30 proviruses; 25 touch a contig edge and 11 carry an integrase gene.

seq_name
Provirus identifier, built as source_seq|provirus_start_end (1-based, inclusive coordinates).
source_seq
The assembly contig the provirus was found in.
start / end
Coordinates of the provirus region on source_seq.
length
Length of the provirus region, in bp.
n_genes
Number of genes geNomad predicted inside the provirus.
v_vs_c_score
Virus-versus-cellular score — the strength of viral over cellular hallmark evidence (higher = more confidently viral). Here they range 8.1–175.2.
in_seq_edge
True when the provirus starts or ends at the edge of its contig, i.e. it is probably partial. 25 of the 30 are edge cases.
integrases
Gene(s) identified as integrases (seq_name_<n>, semicolon-separated), or NA when none was found.

Companion files: gene calls, taxonomy and sequence-search hits for these proviruses are in human_gut_assembly_provirus_genes.tsv, ..._taxonomy.tsv and ..._mmseqs2.tsv.
